ChatGPT arrives in Microsoft Word across all plans

OpenAI has added ChatGPT to Microsoft Word, extending its Office integration beyond Excel and PowerPoint and putting document drafting and revision directly inside Word.

What changed

OpenAI says ChatGPT is now available in Microsoft Word through the same Microsoft add-in used for its other Office integrations. From the Word sidebar, users can draft from notes, summarize documents, revise selected text, and adjust headings and formatting.

The Word integration is available across ChatGPT plans, including Free. OpenAI says plan limits still apply, and usage can draw from shared allowances for premium features on plans where those limits are shared.

Why it matters

For buyers comparing AI writing tools, the important change is workflow placement. ChatGPT can now operate where many business documents are already created rather than requiring users to move text between a browser assistant and Word. That can reduce friction for drafting, editing and document cleanup, especially in organizations already standardized on Microsoft 365.

Buyer takeaway

This makes native workflow integration a more important comparison point when evaluating general AI assistants against dedicated writing products. Buyers should still compare governance, organizational controls, usage limits and document context handling, but teams that spend much of their working day in Word now have a more direct reason to include ChatGPT in that evaluation.
