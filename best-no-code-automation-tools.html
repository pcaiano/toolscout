What to compare

For no-code automation, compare platforms first on the workflows you actually need to run: simple triggers and actions, multi-step logic, branching, data transformation, approvals or self-hosted control. A platform that is easy for one workflow can become restrictive when automations grow in complexity.

Integration coverage matters only when it matches the apps in your stack. Also compare how workflows are debugged, maintained and priced as task volume increases, because a low-friction setup can become expensive or difficult to manage at scale.
