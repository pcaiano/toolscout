What to compare

For a sales team, CRM fit depends on whether sellers can keep pipeline data current without adding excessive administration. Compare opportunity ownership, pipeline views, activity capture and follow-up automation before treating advanced reporting as the deciding feature.

Managers may need forecasting, permissions and repeatable reporting while individual sellers need fast daily workflows. The best CRM for a sales team balances those needs and integrates with the email, prospecting and automation systems already used by the team.
