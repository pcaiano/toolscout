Best Affordable CRM Software

Affordable CRM software should cover the essentials without forcing a small business to pay for features it does not need. Start with budget, then compare fit.

What to compare

  • Free plan or low-cost entry point
  • Core contact and pipeline management
  • Automation and integrations
  • Ease of setup and daily use
  • How pricing changes as the team grows

Good starting points

HubSpot has free tools available and covers CRM, sales, marketing and service.

Pipedrive is focused on sales pipeline and deal management.
