Brevo profile

Marketing and customer communication platform for email, SMS, automation and transactional messaging.

ToolScout view

Brevo deserves consideration when storage and sending capacity are different business problems. Its free plan permits up to 100,000 stored contacts but only 300 email sends per day. This is attractive for a contact-heavy operation running small daily campaigns, yet a launch announcement to thousands of recipients requires scheduling or a paid plan. The same free tier limits automation entry to 2,000 unique contacts and retains Brevo branding on messages. The practical comparison with Mailchimp is therefore not simply which offers more features; it is which usage limit the team will encounter first. Brevo fits contact-rich, controlled-cadence workflows, while Mailchimp may suit simpler audience campaigns.

Before you choose: Calculate the largest campaign, daily delivery need, transactional send volume and how many contacts enter automations. Editorial review 2026-10-08.
