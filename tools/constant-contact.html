Constant Contact

Email and digital marketing platform for campaigns, automation, events, lead generation and small-business marketing.

ToolScout view

Constant Contact documents an Automation Path Builder triggered by subscriber actions, calendar dates and commerce activity. Its help centre distinguishes current automation paths from earlier legacy email series, a meaningful issue for migrating users. Newsletter-only teams may not need complicated journeys; event-led marketers should verify entitlements, consent controls and audience segments first. Price the workflow the team actually uses rather than all available features.

Before you choose: Build a list-join and purchase follow-up path while testing subscription consent. Editorial assessment 2026-10-08. First-party documentation informed this editorial assessment.
