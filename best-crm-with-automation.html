Best CRM with Automation in 2026

CRM automation is useful when it removes repetitive work: assigning follow-ups, updating deal stages, sending reminders and keeping customer records consistent.

HubSpot

A strong option when you want CRM automation alongside broader marketing, sales and service capabilities.

Useful CRM automation

  • Automatic follow-up reminders.
  • Lead and deal assignment.
  • Pipeline stage updates.
  • Notifications when important actions are overdue.

How to choose

Start with the repetitive tasks that cost you time today. A CRM is useful when automation makes those tasks reliable without creating a complicated workflow that needs constant maintenance.
