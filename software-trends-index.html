Methodology and limits

  • Named tools are included only when their ToolScout factual record is within 45 days, their official HTTPS source is currently healthy, and that source was checked within 10 days.
  • Search-demand figures come from the Google Search Console Search Analytics data already ingested by ToolScout. Impressions are visibility, not visits or market demand estimates.
  • Shortlist frequency counts appearances in current ToolScout guide shortlists after category, structured capability, hard attribute, relevance and trust gates. It is an editorial coverage measure, not user popularity.
  • Affiliate status, commission and payout do not increase editorial eligibility, shortlist frequency or ranking.

Excluded from named analysis: 9 catalog record(s) that did not pass all strict source gates at publication time. This exclusion is intentional.
