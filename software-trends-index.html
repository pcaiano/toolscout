Signal to watch

AI agents are becoming reusable team workflows

Recent product releases point toward reusable skills, shared AI behaviour and repeatable agent workflows instead of isolated prompting.

Signal to watch

MCP and related integration layers are making software easier for AI tools to read from and act on with explicit permissions.
