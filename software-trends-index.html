Method

Primary sources first.

ToolScout selects material product changes from official vendor documentation and public product updates, classifies the buyer impact, then aggregates the selected evidence into trend categories. Affiliate status never changes inclusion, weighting or prominence.

The index measures a rolling 30 day editorial sample only. It does not claim global market share, adoption, revenue or user sentiment.
