Best Free CRM for Small Business in 2026

Choosing a free CRM is less about finding the longest feature list and more about finding a system a small team can actually adopt and keep using.

Our shortlist

HubSpot

A strong starting point for teams that want a broad CRM and room to add sales, marketing and service workflows.

Pipedrive

A sales-focused option worth considering when pipeline visibility and deal management matter more than having a broad all-in-one platform.

Explore Pipedrive

What should a small business look for?

  • A genuinely usable free tier, not just a short trial.
  • Simple contact and deal management.
  • Room to automate repetitive sales work as the team grows.
  • Clear upgrade paths when the free plan stops being enough.

HubSpot or Pipedrive?

For a broader customer platform, HubSpot is the more natural starting point. For a sales-led team that wants a focused pipeline, Pipedrive may be the better fit.
