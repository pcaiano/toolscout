- Manufacturer guide 2: https://www.guesty.com/features/channel-manager/airbnb/

### Hostaway
- Home: https://www.hostaway.com/
- Manufacturer guide 1: https://www.hostaway.com/features/property-management-system/
- Manufacturer guide 2: https://www.hostaway.com/features/channel-manager/

- Boundary: pricing is quote/portfolio driven; no verified permanent free plan or exact comparable monthly cost in this research.
- Buyer check: obtain a written commercial quote at the intended number of properties and confirm channel and operational permissions.

### Lodgify
- Home: https://www.lodgify.com/
- Manufacturer guide 1: https://www.lodgify.com/all-features/
- Manufacturer guide 2: https://www.lodgify.com/vacation-rental-channel-manager/

- Buyer check: verify property count, channel/booking engine terms, payment processor costs and checkout price for Portugal.
