# ToolScout affiliate policy

ToolScout may earn commissions when users click approved affiliate links and purchase qualifying products or services.

## Rules

1. Never invent an affiliate relationship or commission.
2. Only add an affiliate URL after the relevant program has approved ToolScout.
3. Keep product facts sourced from the provider or another authoritative source.
4. Show useful comparison and recommendation logic independent of commission size.
5. Do not rank a tool higher solely because it pays more.
6. Clearly disclose affiliate relationships on pages containing affiliate links.
7. Use `rel="nofollow sponsored noopener"` for commercial outbound links.
8. Record verification dates for pricing, features and affiliate terms.

The goal is to build a recommendation product users can trust, not a thin affiliate directory.
