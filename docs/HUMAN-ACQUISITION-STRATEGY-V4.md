# ToolScout Human Acquisition v4

Effective: 2026-09-24

## Objective

Turn ToolScout into an always-on, semi-passive machine for qualified human acquisition without turning Cloudflare load, external actions, backlinks or generated pages into vanity goals.

The commercial chain is:

`GA4 sessions -> strict attribution-quality humans -> verified outbound -> monetized outbound -> confirmed revenue`

GA4 is the canonical headline traffic population. Strict-human evidence is the channel-quality and exact-attribution layer.

## Allocation

- 60%: existing search demand and software decision intent.
- 25%: relevant authority, publisher distribution and vendor amplification.
- 10%: AI/AEO/agent discovery where marginal cost is low.
- 5%: Growth R&D for genuinely new acquisition mechanisms.

The percentages are prioritization weights, not spend commitments.

## Search demand

A Demand Map comes before new SEO surface creation. Prefer comparisons, alternatives, best-for, best-free, use-case and high-intent tool profiles. Existing GSC evidence outranks heuristic opportunity. New programmatic pages require semantic eligibility and factual editorial value.

## Competitive acquisition intelligence

Continuously compare ToolScout with Futurepedia, FutureTools, Toolify, There's An AI For That, Aixploria, TopAI.tools, Dynamite AI and established software directories. Extract gaps in referring domains, distribution surfaces, decision-page formats and vendor amplification. A competitor backlink is only a research signal until ToolScout verifies legitimacy, audience fit and a non-paid route.

## Distribution and vendor loop

Scale a surface only after verified human or commercial evidence. Public placement or backlink count alone is insufficient. Repeated routes with activity but no human yield are rotated or suppressed. Vendor amplification remains informational and editorially independent.

## Kill / scale learning

- Scale: repeated strict attributed humans, outbound, monetized outbound or confirmed revenue.
- Measure: one-off or directional human evidence and newly live placements.
- Explore: only bounded high-signal opportunities.
- Rotate or suppress: mature repeated actions with zero human yield.
- Paid distribution never auto-scales.

## Resource budgets

The machine is always on, but work is bursty and bounded. Current per-cycle budgets are 12 qualification checks, 4 automatic external executions, 16 network candidates, 6 contact scans, 8 route actions, 6 discovery fetches and 6 automatic submissions. Authority target is 8 qualified attempts per 24h; external-action target is 8 with a hard operating budget of 12 per 24h. These are ceilings and targets, never activity quotas.

## Command Center

Success is read in this order: GA4 sessions, strict attribution-quality humans, verified outbound, monetized outbound, confirmed revenue. Search visibility and relevant referring domains are leading indicators. External executions, internal cycles and queue size are operational diagnostics only.

## Reliability contract

No engine may increase fan-out simply because traffic is low. When human yield is absent, it must change the opportunity mix before increasing work. Unchanged state is not rewritten where avoidable; D1 batching remains bounded; paid, CAPTCHA, account-creation and irreversible third-party actions remain human-gated.
