# ToolScout — External Distribution Playbook

## Objective

Create the first qualified external discovery loops for ToolScout without buying traffic or relying on spam. Prioritize channels that can produce discovery, backlinks, feedback, or durable search visibility.

## Positioning

**ToolScout — Find the right AI & software tool for the job.**

ToolScout helps people choose software and AI tools based on the job they need to accomplish, their constraints and workflow — rather than generic popularity rankings.

Primary URL: https://trytoolscout.org/

> ToolScout helps you find the right AI or software tool for the job. Instead of generic rankings, it starts with what you're trying to accomplish and recommends tools that fit the workflow, constraints and budget.

> We built ToolScout because choosing software has become a search problem. Tell us the job, and ToolScout narrows the field.

> We built ToolScout around a simple idea: the best tool isn't the most popular one — it's the one that fits the job. We're starting with a curated catalog, deterministic recommendations and transparent outbound links. We'd love to learn where the recommendations are useful and where they miss.

## Distribution content sequence

1. Product Hunt launch — broad product story.
2. SaaSHub / AlternativeTo / Alternative.me — directory discovery.
3. Publish one genuinely useful buying guide per week around the strongest commercial intents.
4. Share the specific guide when participating in an existing relevant discussion; never drop links without context.
5. Use analytics to identify which intent receives external traffic and clicks.
6. Double down on winners and stop producing weak topics.

## First SEO/distribution themes

Prioritize topics already supported by ToolScout's commercial-intent model and catalog:

- Best marketing automation tools
- Best CRM for real estate
- Best CRM for consultants
- Best affordable CRM
- Best SEO tools
- Best competitor SEO tools

These are strategic priorities, not claims of search volume. Actual demand must come from observed analytics/search data.

## Measurement

For every external source, measure:

- sessions
- searches
- outbound clicks
- affiliate-covered clicks
- unmonetized commercial clicks
- conversion quality where observable

Never fabricate revenue. An outbound click is not a sale.

## Rules

- No purchased votes or fake engagement.
- No spam submissions.
- No fabricated reviews or claims.
- No fake search-volume claims.
- Keep affiliate disclosure clear.
- Use the canonical public domain: https://trytoolscout.org/
