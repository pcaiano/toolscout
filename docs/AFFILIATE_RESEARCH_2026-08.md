# Affiliate shortlist — August 2026

This is a research shortlist, not a claim that ToolScout has been accepted into any program. Exact terms must be confirmed at application and before publishing links.

## Priority A

### HubSpot
- Category: CRM / sales / marketing
- Published affiliate structure: 30% recurring for up to one year; 180-day cookie reported on current program materials.
- Why it fits ToolScout: strong overlap with CRM, small-business and marketing intent.
- Official program: https://www.hubspot.com/partners/affiliates
- Status in ToolScout: disabled until approved and tracking URL is supplied.

### Semrush
- Category: SEO / search visibility / marketing
- Published affiliate structure: $100–$300 per sale plus $10 for eligible free trials; 120-day cookie on current official affiliate page.
- Why it fits ToolScout: high commercial intent for SEO-tool searches and strong standalone landing-page potential.
- Official program: https://www.semrush.com/lp/affiliate-program/en/
- Status in ToolScout: disabled until approved and tracking URL is supplied.

### Pipedrive
- Category: CRM / sales
- Official affiliate page confirms no sign-up fee, no minimum sales requirements and no earnings cap; tiered affiliate structure applies.
- Why it fits ToolScout: direct match for CRM and sales-intent queries.
- Official program: https://www.pipedrive.com/en/affiliate-partnership
- Status in ToolScout: disabled until approved and tracking URL is supplied.

## Ranking rule

Affiliate commission is never a recommendation score. ToolScout ranks products on user fit first. Affiliate economics are used only to prioritize which programs to apply for and integrate.
