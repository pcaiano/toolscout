# Snippet and Answer-First Optimization — 2026-09-06

This change targets existing decision-context guides that have early Google Search Console signals. It does not create new URLs or change ranking logic.

## What changes

- title tags become clearer comparison-oriented snippets;
- meta descriptions are generated from the page's persona, job-to-be-done and constraints;
- opening lead copy becomes answer-first and decision-oriented;
- Open Graph title/description stay aligned with search metadata.

## Guardrails

- no keyword stuffing;
- no unsupported superlatives, review counts, performance statistics or testing claims;
- no change to canonical URLs;
- no change to affiliate routing or editorial scoring;
- no claim that low-sample GSC positions are stable rankings;
- keep snippet copy human-readable and useful to answer engines.

## Measurement

Evaluate over time using GSC impressions, clicks and CTR by page, while remembering that current samples are small. Do not infer causality from a handful of impressions.
