Waiting for signals

The matrix ranks observed demand, outbound interest, commercial value, current affiliate routing coverage and SEO opportunity. It never treats estimated value as actual revenue.

Decision rules

How to read
Observed clicks to tools without a currently active affiliate route = affiliate coverage priority. High SEO score + no observed demand = content/SEO priority. High demand + low clicks = product/page optimisation. Owner activity is excluded when explicitly marked. Historical click-time monetization is not rewritten when current affiliate status changes.
