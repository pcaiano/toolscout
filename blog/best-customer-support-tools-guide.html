The Shortlist for Customer Support Tools: Features, Trade-offs and Fit

What to compare when evaluating customer support tools, with emphasis on workflow fit, price, setup effort and long-term usefulness.

Choosing between customer support tools tools is usually less about finding the longest feature list and more about identifying which product creates the least friction for your team.

Where will this sit in the workflow?

Map the tool to the step before it and the step after it. Integration quality, hand-offs and ownership often matter more than standalone feature depth.

Separate must-haves from attractive extras

A useful shortlist should survive without decorative features. Prioritise the capabilities tied directly to the result you need and treat everything else as a tie-breaker.
