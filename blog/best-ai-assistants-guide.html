Choosing an AI assistant by the work you actually do

A concise ToolScout field note on ai assistants, focused on fit, trade-offs and the differences that change the buying decision.

The useful question in ai assistants is not which product has the longest feature list. It is which differences will matter in the workflow you actually have.

Start with the job

Write down the outcome, who will use the software, what it must connect to and what would make adoption fail. That removes many plausible-but-wrong options.

Find the expensive compromise

Compare implementation effort, integrations, pricing and the first limitation you are likely to hit. The cheapest subscription is not always the cheapest decision.
