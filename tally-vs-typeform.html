What this comparison means in practice

Typeform's strongest score advantage is research, at 9 versus 5. Tally answers with smaller advantages led by price. Tally adds payments and logic to the comparison, while Typeform brings lead capture and analytics. Their recorded audiences differ enough that the buyer profile should carry real weight in the decision. ToolScout has not yet verified AI assistant or agent interoperability for either product, so this factor should not be used to choose between them yet.

Decision: Choose Tally when price matters more and the buyer looks like startups; choose Typeform when research is the harder requirement and marketing teams is the closer operating context.
