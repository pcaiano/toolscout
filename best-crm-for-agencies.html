Best CRM for Agencies

Agency CRM selection is about more than storing contacts. The best fit helps a team manage multiple client pipelines, follow-ups, sales activity and repeatable workflows without excessive administration.

What matters for agencies

  • Separate or flexible client pipelines
  • Lead and deal visibility across the team
  • Automation for follow-ups and handoffs
  • Integrations with marketing and communication tools
  • Reporting that can support agency growth

Good starting points

HubSpot — broader customer platform with CRM, sales, marketing and service capabilities.

Pipedrive — sales-focused CRM suited to teams that want a straightforward pipeline and deal workflow.
