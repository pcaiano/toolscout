Decision guide / CRM

Best CRM for agencies.

Agency CRM selection is about more than storing contacts. The right fit keeps multiple pipelines, follow-ups and team handoffs visible without adding unnecessary administration.

What matters

Start with the operating model.

  • Flexible client and sales pipelines.
  • Lead and deal visibility across the team.
  • Automation for follow-ups and handoffs.
  • Integrations with marketing and communication tools.
  • Reporting that supports agency growth.
