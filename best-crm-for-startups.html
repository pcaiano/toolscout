Best CRM for Startups

Startups need a CRM that can grow with the team without adding unnecessary complexity. The right choice usually depends on sales process, budget, automation and how quickly the team needs to adopt it.

What to look for

  • Fast setup and easy adoption
  • Clear pipeline and deal management
  • Useful automation without heavy admin
  • Integrations with the tools you already use
  • A pricing model that can scale with the team

Start with these CRM options

HubSpot — broad CRM, sales, marketing and service coverage with free tools available.

Pipedrive — focused sales CRM built around pipelines, deals and sales activity.
