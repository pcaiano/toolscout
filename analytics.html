How to read this

ToolScout currently measures on-site behaviour: sessions, searches, intents and outbound clicks. Affiliate programmes do not expose vendor-side sales automatically, so this dashboard does not fabricate revenue. “Monetized clicks” means clicks on tools that currently have a real personal affiliate URL active in ToolScout; “unmonetized clicks” are commercial opportunities where a link or programme is not active yet.
