What should “free” actually include?

  • Enough landing or funnel pages to test a real offer.
  • Email follow-up, rather than a disconnected page builder.
  • A path to capture leads and move them toward an offer.
  • A clear upgrade path when the free plan becomes too small.

Who should start with a free funnel builder?

A free plan makes sense when you are validating an offer, launching your first lead magnet or learning how funnels work. It is less suitable when you already need advanced automation, complex integrations or large-scale campaign operations.
